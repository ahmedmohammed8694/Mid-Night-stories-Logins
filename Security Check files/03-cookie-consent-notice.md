## 3. Your Consent Choices

When you first visit the Service, you'll see a cookie banner allowing you to:
- **Accept all** cookies
- **Reject non-essential** cookies (only strictly necessary cookies will be used)
- **Customize** preferences by category

Your choice is remembered for [12 months], after which you'll be asked again. You can change your preferences anytime via **[Cookie Settings link in footer]**.

> **Design requirement:** The banner must offer a genuine, equally prominent "Reject" option — not just an "OK" button with no real opt-out. Pre-ticked boxes for non-essential cookies are not valid consent under GDPR/ePrivacy rules.
