Accessibility Statement

Digital Inclusion, Screen-Reader Compatibility & WCAG Compliance

Last updated: August 2026

1. Commitment to Accessibility

Midnight Stories is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continuously improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards based on the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

2. Accessibility Features Implemented

  • Keyboard Navigation: All interactive buttons, forms, and navigation menus are accessible via keyboard tab navigation with visible focus indicators.
  • Semantic HTML Structure: Heading hierarchy (H1, H2, H3), landmark roles (<header>, <main>, <footer>), and descriptive aria-label attributes for assistive technologies.
  • High Contrast & Themes: High-contrast color palettes in both dark and light modes, meeting minimum contrast ratio requirements (4.5:1 for normal text).
  • Image Alt Text & EXIF Stripping: Uploaded images are stripped of metadata while requiring or providing structural alt attributes for screen readers.
  • Responsive Design: Layouts adapt dynamically across desktop, tablet, and mobile displays without horizontal scroll overflow.

3. Feedback & Assistance Channels

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of Midnight Stories. If you encounter accessibility barriers, please inform us so we can resolve the issue promptly:

  • Email: support@midnightstories.dpdns.org
  • Support Ticket Portal: https://midnightstories.dpdns.org/support
