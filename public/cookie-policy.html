Cookie Policy

Transparent Cookie Classification & Consent Management

Last updated: August 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit websites. They help websites remember preferences, maintain secure user sessions, and enhance user experience.

2. Categorization of Cookies Used on Midnight Stories

2.1 Essential & Functional Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are required for fundamental platform functionality and cannot be disabled in our systems:

  • theme: Stores your UI color theme preference (dark vs. light mode).
  • banner_state: Remembers whether you dismissed the emergency crisis resource notification.
  • auth_token: Secure session token for logged-in user authentication and CSRF protection.

2.2 Analytical & Performance Cookies

Currently, Midnight Stories does not employ cross-site tracking or third-party behavioral analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics or Meta Pixel are not used).

2.3 Marketing & Advertising Cookies

Midnight Stories is an ad-free creative platform. We do not use marketing, targeting, or retargeting cookies.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Deleting essential cookies may affect your logged-in state or site preference persistence.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding our cookie practices, please contact privacy@midnightstories.dpdns.org.
