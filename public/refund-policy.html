Cancellation & Refund Policy

Voluntary Donations, Account Cancellation & Subscription Terms

Last updated: August 2026

1. Free Platform Access

Midnight Stories (https://midnightstories.dpdns.org) is fundamentally a free creative platform. Browsing stories, reading books in our library, submitting anonymous stories, and commenting on public works do not require any paid subscription, fee, or payment registration.

2. Billing & Subscriptions (Placeholder Terms)

While the platform is currently free, we support a 💳 Billing & Subscriptions help ticket category to prepare for potential future premium features (such as professional formatting tools, ad-free reader upgrades, or voluntary recurring support plans). If recurring subscriptions are introduced in the future:

  • Billing Cycle: Subscriptions will be billed in advance on a recurring monthly or annual basis and are non-refundable except as explicitly provided herein.
  • Cancellation: You may cancel your subscription renewal at any time. Upon cancellation, your premium access will continue until the end of the active billing period, at which point it will terminate.
  • No Pro-Rata Refunds: Except as required by local consumer protection regulations (such as EU/UK statutory cooling-off periods), we do not provide pro-rata refunds for unused portions of a billing cycle.

3. Voluntary Contributions & Donations

In the event that the platform implements direct tipping or voluntary donations to support indie authors or server infrastructure, please be aware that all tips, contributions, and donations are voluntary, final, and 100% non-refundable.

4. EU/UK Right of Withdrawal (Cooling-off Period)

If you are a consumer residing in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have a statutory right to cancel a digital subscription service contract within 14 days of purchase without giving any reason. If you exercise this right, we will refund any payments received from you, minus a proportionate charge representing the service provided up until the time you notified us of your cancellation.

5. Account Deletion & Data Erasure Impact

If you choose to delete your account or exercise your GDPR "Right to be Forgotten" via the settings or by filing a support ticket, any active subscription associated with your account will be immediately terminated. No refunds will be issued for any remaining active subscription time at the point of account erasure.

6. Refund Inquiries & Dispute Resolution

For any billing concerns, payment failures, or statutory refund requests, please open a help ticket under the Billing & Subscriptions category on our support portal or email us directly:
