Privacy Policy

Comprehensive Data Protection, User Rights & Regulatory Compliance

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview & Lawful Basis for Processing

Midnight Stories ("the Platform", "we", "us", or "our") operates the website located at https://midnightstories.dpdns.org. We are committed to protecting user privacy, securing Personally Identifiable Information (PII), and fulfilling global regulatory mandates including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), India's Information Technology Act 2000 / DPDP Act 2023, and Canada's PIPEDA.

We process personal data based on explicit lawful grounds (GDPR Art. 6):

  • Consent: When you voluntarily register, submit stories, or enable non-essential cookies.
  • Contractual Necessity: To fulfill account creation, authenticate credentials, and deliver platform features.
  • Legitimate Interests: To protect platform integrity, prevent abuse, moderate content, and maintain cybersecurity.
  • Legal Compliance: To satisfy statutory obligations under applicable law.

2. Categorization of Collected Data

2.1 Automatically Collected Data

  • Hashed & Raw IP Address: Cryptographically hashed for rate-limiting and abuse mitigation. Raw IPs may be temporarily processed in memory for threat screening before hashing.
  • Technical Identifiers: Browser user-agent strings, device operating system parameters, and connection timestamps.
  • Essential Functional Cookies: Session tokens, theme settings (dark/light), and crisis-banner dismissal states.

2.2 Non-Automatically Collected PII

  • User Account Profile: Full name, email address, salted/hashed password (via bcryptjs), Google OAuth ID (if sign-in via Google is selected), date of birth (dob), phone number, profile picture, and bio.
  • User Generated Content: Submitted life stories, book manuscripts, comments, support ticket logs, attachments, and real-time chat messages.
  • Preferences & Interaction Data: Privacy toggles (e.g., show_email, show_phone), direct message permissions, and reading activity/bookmarks.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing & Sub-Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We engage vetted service providers for critical operations under strict data processing agreements:

  • Cloud Infrastructure & Database: Cloudflare Workers & D1 Distributed Database (Hosting, API routing, and encrypted state management).
  • Authentication Providers: Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0 authentication).
  • Automated Content Safety: AI-assisted moderation engines for detecting hate speech and severe violations (data processed ephemerally without persistent marketing profiling).

4. Global User Rights

4.1 Core Rights (GDPR / PIPEDA / DPDP)

Subject to applicable regional laws, users maintain the following rights:

  • Right to Access & Rectification: Request a copy of your personal data or update inaccurate details in your user profile.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Request permanent account and content deletion.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain your submitted content in a structured, machine-readable JSON format.
  • Right to Restriction & Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or request processing restrictions.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a grievance with your local Data Protection Authority.

4.2 US Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to request disclosure of categories of PII collected, request deletion, and mandate non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights. Midnight Stories does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Framework)

Midnight Stories is strictly intended for individuals aged 13 and older (or 16 in certain EU jurisdictions). We do not knowingly solicit or collect PII from children under 13. If we discover a registered user is under the legal age limit without verified parental consent, the account and associated data will be immediately purged.

6. Security & Data Retention

  • Security Measures: Data at rest is encrypted, passwords are hashed using salt rounds via bcryptjs, and two-factor authentication (2FA/MFA) is supported.
  • Account & Content Retention: Retained while your account remains active. Upon deletion, data is removed from primary D1 database tables within 30 days.
  • Security & Audit Logs: Hashed IP logs and access audit trails are automatically purged after 90 days.
